# Third-party content and rights

## Three.js

Three.js r180 is vendored in `public/vendor/`. Its MIT license is retained at [public/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt](public/vendor/THREE-LICENSE.txt).

## Data

The normalized snapshot contains public BLS observations and Crossref bibliographic metadata. Each collection retains its source URL, coverage, licensing notes, and provenance in `public/assets/universe.json` and `unreal/Content/Data/source-records.json`. No publication full texts or abstracts are included. Provider metadata access does not grant rights to the underlying works.

## Supplied media

`public/media/cyberalpine-header.mp4` is the user-supplied Runway timeline export, remuxed for progressive web playback without re-encoding. Its JPEG poster is extracted from the same film. The media is not covered by the Three.js license.

## Project code and assets

No blanket open-source license has been selected for the original Cyberalpine project code or media. Their respective rights holders retain their rights.
